# 📊 Featured Data Analytics & BI Projects

### 📊 Revenue Performance Analysis (SQL, Excel, Power BI) [🔗 Link](https://github.com/zandernh/Revenue-Performance-Analysis)

![Revenue Performance Screenshot](Images/revenue_performance_analysis_screenshot.png)

End-to-end revenue analytics project focused on identifying geographic revenue concentration, seasonal trends, and business growth opportunities using SQL, Excel, Power BI, and executive reporting techniques.

- Tech Stack: PostgreSQL • SQL • Excel • Power BI • Power Query • DAX • PDF Reporting

- Highlights: SQL data aggregation, Excel exploratory analysis, Power BI dashboard developement, DAX KPI calculations, Regional revenue modelling, business reporting, interactive slicers & KPI cards.

#### Key Insights:
- United Kingdom contributed ~84% of total revenue
- Top 5 countries generated ~97% of total revenue
- Strong Q4 seasonal revenue trend identified
- Revenue heavily concentrated in a small number of markets

### 📊 Customer Value Analysis (SQL, Excel, Power BI) [🔗 Link](https://github.com/zandernh/Customer-Value-Analysis)

![Customer Value Analysis Screenshot](Images/customer_value_analysis_screenshot.png)

End-to-end customer analytics project focused on customer segmentation, retention analysis, purchasing behavior, and revenue concentration using SQL, Excel, Power BI, and business reporting techniques.

- Tech Stack: PostgreSQL • SQL • Excel • Power BI • Power Query • PDF Reporting

- Highlights: SQL CTEs & CASE statements, customer segmentation modelling, Excel exploratory analysis, Power BI dashboard development, customer retention analysis, interactive filtering, business reporting & data storytelling.

#### Key Insights:
- Repeat customers generated ~93% of total revenue
- Top 10 customers contributed ~17% of total revenue
- Revenue heavily concentrated among loyal high-value customers
- High Segment customers generated significantly higher revenue per customer
